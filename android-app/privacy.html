🏠 Gingerbread House Workshop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Gingerbread House Workshop (the “app” and the website at this address) is a game for building and decorating a 3D gingerbread house on your own device. This policy explains what information is involved when you use it.

We do not collect your data

The game does not require an account, does not include ads or analytics, and does not send your houses, photos, name, or anything else to us or to any third party. There is no sign-in, no tracking, and no crash or usage reporting built into the app or the website.

Your houses stay on your device

  • Autosave and “My saves” are stored locally in your browser or in the app (using the device’s local storage). They stay on that device unless you delete them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Save (💾) writes a .gingerbread file that you choose to keep or share. On the Android app this uses the system share sheet, so you pick where the file goes (Files, Drive, a messaging app, and so on).
  • Load (📂) opens a file you choose with the system file picker. The file is read on your device to rebuild your house and is not uploaded.
  • Photo (📷) creates an image of your house on your device. You can save or share that image yourself.

If you share a file or photo with someone else, or save it to a cloud folder you control, that share is your choice and follows that service’s own policy.

Name caption

The optional name you type is used only to label your house and photo on your device. It is stored with your local save and included on a photo if you take one. It is not sent to us.

Internet permission

The Android app may declare an Internet permission because it is a standard part of the container that displays the game. The game itself runs offline from files bundled in the app and does not need a network connection to play, save, load, or take photos. We do not use this permission to collect or transmit your data.

Device backups

Your device or platform (for example Android Backup or a browser sync feature you turned on) may include the app’s local storage in a backup you already use. That is controlled by you and by your device maker or account, not by a service we run.

Children

The game is a casual family activity and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and post the new policy at this same URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer of Gingerbread House Workshop through the app’s store listing or the GitHub repository for this project: github.com/Diebeste/gingerbread-workshop.
